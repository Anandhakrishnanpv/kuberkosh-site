About Us · Who we are

Supply you can plan around.

KuberKosh Industrial Solutions is an industrial material and equipment supplier based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, trading electrical equipment and mechanical switchgear in India and abroad. Our portfolio covers motors, switchgear, fuses, relays, circuit breakers, safety equipment, pumps, flow meters, actuators, transformers, wires, cables and a wide range of other industrial components. One team handles your enquiry from the first part number to final delivery, with clear pricing and dependable service.

Every business depends on consistent quality and timely delivery. We are committed to delivering both, on every order.
